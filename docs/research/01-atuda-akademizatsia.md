# עתודה אקדמית and אקדמיזציה (research, 2026-10-08)

Primary official sources: the IDF Meitav brochure "העתודה האקדמית, מידעון למועמדים לשנת הלימודים תשפ"ז 2026-2027" (digitaler.cld.bz/atuda2026-2027, abbreviated **[Brochure 26-27]**), the live mitgaisim.idf.il עתודה pages, HUJI/BGU/Technion program pages, and he.wikipedia (only where no official page was reachable, flagged). atuda.org.il returned HTTP 403. No 2027-2028 brochure exists yet.

## 1. עתודה אקדמית: general

- **What it is.** A pre-service academic track: the candidate gets דחיית שירות (דח"ש) to complete a degree, then serves compulsory service as an academic officer plus קבע. Graduates become קצינים מקצועיים אקדמאים (קמ"א). Source: https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/עתודה-אקדמית/ ; [Brochure 26-27].
- **Eligibility.** All י"ב students in the cohort regardless of age; the questionnaire opens automatically only for מלש"בים who meet נתוני קצונה. Medical profile is generally not a barrier except disqualifying profiles. Source: https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/כתבות/עתודה/עתודה/הרשמה-למסלול-העתודה-האקדמית/ (01.01.2024); [Brochure 26-27].
- **Screening data used.** קב"א, דפ"ר and psychometric. Source: https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/כתבות/כתבות-ציר-הזמן/מיון-מתקדם/מיונים-לתפקידי-עורף/מיון-עתודה-אקדמית/ (22.09.2016, old). The usual דפ"ר floor cited by universities is 60 (70 for elite programs); JCT also cites קב"א 52+ (https://www.jct.ac.il/מועמדים/עתודה-אקדמית/, undated). The IDF brochure prints a דפ"ר number only for elite programs (70).
- **Psychometric.** Mandatory; minimum 590 (רב-תחומי) for the regular track; higher per program. Rare exception: admission without psychometric on an outstanding bagrut, universities only. Sources: mitgaisim עתודה page; [Brochure 26-27].
- **Registration timeline for the תשפ"ז cycle (י"ב in 2025-26).** Questionnaire open July to 28.2.2026; psychometric registration confirmation uploaded to Meitav by 28.2; last recommended psychometric April 2026; online personality screening (questionnaire + psychologist interview) December 2025 to June 2026; institution registration only after Meitav approval letter; final committee by end of September 2026; יום חיול in October 2026, then דח"ש. Source: [Brochure 26-27]; mitgaisim עתודה page.
- **Current cycle (י"ב 2026-27, studies start October 2027).** By the annual pattern the questionnaire opened July 2026 and should close 28.2.2027, but **no 2027-2028 brochure confirming this was found**. Inferred.
- **Deferral year.** Candidates going to שנת שירות, מכינה or ישיבת הסדר (full-year programs only) are screened in י"ב and start עתודה after the deferral year. Source: הרשמה page (01.01.2024).
- **Choice limits.** Up to two tracks, up to three institutions per track; the approved field is the field you finish; switching fields only in exceptional cases. Source: [Brochure 26-27]; הרשמה page.
- **Funding model.** The IDF does **not** pay tuition in the regular track. Students receive מענק עידוד עתודה of 10,500 ₪ per study year (conditional on academic standing), a one-time 2,500 ₪ reimbursement for psychometric/registration costs, free public transport, "מועדון המאה" excellence grant 5,000 ₪ (not for elite-program students), mentoring grant up to 5,000 ₪ for years 3-4. Full tuition only in specific programs (צמרת, בינה, פסגה nursing, תלפיות, ארזים, חבצלות). Source: [Brochure 26-27]; mitgaisim עתודה page.
- **Commitment.** "בסיום הלימודים האקדמיים תשרתו שירות חובה על פי חוק כקצינים אקדמיים (דין אישה כדין גבר) ושלוש שנות שירות קבע, למעט מסלולים ייחודיים". Officer course during the first year of compulsory service; rank סגן after it; MA possible from second year of service. Source: [Brochure 26-27].
- **Compulsory service length today.** Men: 32 months. The Knesset passed (reported 17.7.2026) a temporary provision keeping 32 months; the planned cut to 30 months is deferred to the June 2029 draft. Women in דין אישה כדין גבר roles (includes עתודאיות) serve the male length. Sources: https://news.walla.co.il/item/3854152 (17.7.2026); earlier 36-month proposal: https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/ska5ugb00a (14.7.2024). The mitgaisim ארזים page states עתודה compulsory service as "שנתיים ושמונה חודשים" (32 months): https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/roles/מסלול-שחקים-ארזים/ .
- **Total timeline, regular track.** 3-4 years degree + 32 months compulsory + 3 years קבע; typical release age 27-28. Source for ages: https://mechina-kda.biu.ac.il/academic_reserve (BIU, undated).
- **Pay during studies.** Only the grants above (no salary; the student is in דח"ש). During service: conscript pay plus officer supplements.
- **Failing or leaving.** Leaving requires IDF consent; since 2018 the relevant head of branch may demand repayment of up to the full grants received (he.wikipedia, unofficial). Official: a request with justification to מדור עתודה, judged case by case. Students who fail an elite program's standards drop to the regular עתודה track. Source: [Brochure 26-27]; https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/כתבות/עתודה/עתודה/תכנית-פסגות/ (24.01.2023).
- **Approved fields (תשפ"ז).** Engineering: electrical/electronics, computer, software (מסלול "אמירים" covers software eng., CS, data engineering), mechanical, industrial and management, materials, chemical, civil and environmental, aeronautics (סילון), architecture; sciences: physics, math, statistics, chemistry, biology, geography, geology; law (HUJI, TAU, BIU, Haifa, Reichman); economics, accounting, business, logistics; behavioral/social sciences (sociology, HR, social work); medicine, dentistry, nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy, emergency medicine. Source: [Brochure 26-27]. HUJI's עתודה page (updated October 2026) adds data science and CS/computational biology, and does **not** list psychology: https://new.huji.ac.il/לימודי-עתודה . Psychology appears in the אקדמיזציה eligible-field list but no 2026 official confirmation of a psychology עתודה quota was found.

## 3. אקדמיזציה and degrees during service

- **Official meaning of מסלול אקדמיזציה.** A track for מלש"בים who already hold (or will hold by enlistment) a bachelor's in an IDF-needed field. Requirements: high נתוני איכות, degree average 75+, full compulsory service (דין אישה כדין גבר), **24 months קבע**, clearance סודי ביותר. Benefits: קמ"א officer rank, placement in the degree field, one-time grant 10,500 ₪, faster promotion. Source: https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/כתבות/ראשי/עתודה/מסלול-אקדמיזציה (27.01.2021); [Brochure 26-27] (interviews January to May, answers June, יום חיול July; deferral to finish a degree requires 50% of credits for a 3-year degree or 30% for a 4-year degree). Eligible fields include CS, all engineering, math, physics, chemistry, biology, economics, law, psychology, medicine, nursing, pharmacy, dentistry.
- **Differences from עתודה.** No grants during the degree; קבע 24 months instead of 36; regular basic training as טוראים before rank. Excellence sub-programs for degree holders: גלים (EE M.Sc.), ברקת (data eng. M.Sc.), ארזים 2 (1-year M.Sc. TAU), מרום (2025, systems eng. M.Sc. Technion), 2023 תקשוב BOOTCAMPs דלתא and אוניקס. Source: https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/מסלול_אקדמיזציה (13.8.2026, unofficial).
- **Degrees earned during service ("מסלול אקדמי ביחידה").** Unit programs where the IDF pays and the soldier is in uniform: תלפיות, חבצלות, ארזים, אלונים (formally עתודה), אדרים, קורס טיס (B.A. from BGU during the course; 7 years קבע since 2015, unofficial), קורס חובלים (2.5 years, B.A. Haifa U., 5 years קבע, profile 82, דפ"ר 60: https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/תפקידים/חובלת/ ), ארז, אודם.
- **ממר"ם / בסמ"ח / תקשוב.** No official in-service funded degree track for draftees was found. Anecdotal: ממר"ם soldiers study at the Open University at their own expense with unit approval (https://www.mako.co.il/pzm-recruits/guides/Article-045dbd660b9ec31006.htm, 19.02.2013, old). Unverified for 2026.
- **"תואר תוך כדי שירות" generally.** Requires unit approval; Open University historically recognized; BIU's המדור לזרועות הביטחון offers tracks aimed at קבע soldiers (13/11/2025): https://mzb.biu.ac.il/studying_during_the_army .

## 4. Common teen misconceptions and the facts

1. **"עתודאים don't really serve as soldiers."** False. Full compulsory service (32 months, דין אישה כדין גבר) as an officer, then 3 years קבע; basic training and officer course in year one.
2. **"You can study whatever you want."** False. Only IDF-approved fields with quotas; no switching after approval except exceptional cases.
3. **"The army pays the tuition."** Mostly false. Regular עתודה gets 10,500 ₪ a year plus 2,500 ₪ once; full tuition only in specific programs.
4. **"You get released at 21."** False. Regular עתודה release around 27-28; תלפיות/חבצלות about 9 years from enlistment.
5. **"You can quit and just pay back the money."** False. Leaving requires IDF consent; repayment may be demanded in addition.
6. **"No psychometric needed."** False for עתודה (590 minimum). True only for תלפיות.
7. **"Your job will match your degree."** Not guaranteed; placement follows IDF needs.
8. **"תלפיות/חבצלות are עתודה."** No; in-service excellence programs with 6 years קבע.
9. **"אקדמיזציה is a way to get a degree during service."** No; it is for people who already have a degree before enlisting (24 months קבע).

- אדרים length: 40 months vs 23 months.
