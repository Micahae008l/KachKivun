# Gibush photo credits

The photos on /gibush. All are openly licensed and taken from Wikimedia Commons; none was taken at a gibush, so each caption on the page says what it really shows. Each is credited next to the image and on /about (`#photo-credits`).
Masters are the Commons 1920px copies (the stretcher is its 1600px original); no other changes. The site serves WebP copies at 640 and 1280px (`<name>-<width>.webp`, made by `python scripts/hero-webp.py`).
